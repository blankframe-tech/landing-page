PoreDekho try-on.

Your shoppers open a link, point their phone's front camera at themselves, and see your earrings or necklace on their own face before they order. It runs in the browser, so there is no app to install.

  • Free pilot for Bangladeshi online jewellery sellers: up to 5 products, 4 to 6 weeks, no charge.
  • We turn your product photos into try-on links you can post on Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp.
  • The camera picture is processed on the shopper's phone. It is never uploaded.
  • This is an early lab project. We want to learn whether try-on cuts returns, and we will share what we find with you.
