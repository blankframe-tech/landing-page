BUILT SPECIFICALLY FOR BIKES

Why cycling and running apps fail motorcyclists.

Motorcycles endure extreme lateral acceleration, rapid lean angles, high-speed braking zones, and strict mechanical upkeep. Generic sports trackers simply don't understand two-wheeled physics.

CORE ARCHITECTURE · 01

Continuous 20Hz Sensor Stream & Motion Calculus

Unlike basic fitness apps that ping GPS every 10–15 seconds, ThrottleIQ streams continuous 20Hz GPS coordinates coupled with phone accelerometer and gyroscope derivatives.

  • Background Wakelock: Keeps recording with foreground service even when phone is screen-locked in a jacket or pocket.
  • Jerk & Acceleration: Computes instantaneous rate of change of acceleration to detect hard braking and aggressive overtakes.
  • Jitter Filtering: Discards poor-accuracy fixes (>25m GPS drift) and cleanly separates idle stops from true moving velocity.

SAFETY NET · 02

Multi-Stage Crash Detection & Emergency Live Link

If a rider goes down on an isolated back road, nobody knows until hours later. ThrottleIQ monitors for crash signatures with mathematical precision.

  • 60-Second Dismiss Countdown: Starts an audible and visual timer to prevent accidental dispatch if you simply dropped your bike.
  • Zero Account Required for Contacts: Up to 5 designated contacts receive an unguessable web link showing live GPS, speed, and phone battery level.
  • No False Alarms: Bump tests, speed bumps, and vibrations do not trigger alerts because both shock spike and speed collapse must co-occur.

ENGINEERING INTEGRITY

Offline-first local SQLite, cloud-synced when ready.

Cellular service drops constantly on mountain twisties and forest roads. ThrottleIQ never drops a packet because the local database is the single source of truth.

On The Bike — Flutter & SQLite

  • Local SQLite (`sqflite`): High-throughput motion points and metadata stored in real transactional SQLite.
  • Riverpod State: Independent providers managing ride recording, motion calculus, and live sync queues.
  • Accuracy Gating: Rejects inaccurate points (>25m GPS spread) to prevent speed spikes.

In The Cloud — Firebase & Cloudinary

  • Firestore Sync: SyncManager detects unsynced records (`synced=0`) on app resume and 5-min intervals.
  • User-Owned Security Rules: Strict Firestore security rules verify rider ownership before allowing writes or queries.
  • Cloudinary CDN: Optimized storage for bike garage photos and community place reviews.
