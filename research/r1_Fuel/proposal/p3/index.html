P3. Boro Decisions — farmers, water sellers and the irrigation cost shock

Question. How do diesel costs, supply uncertainty and a frozen procurement price change farmers’ decisions about how much Boro to plant? Who in the agrarian hierarchy absorbs the cost?

Design. - Sites: 6 villages in 2–3 upazilas, in Rangpur, Rajshahi and Mymensingh (diesel shallow-tube-well heavy), with 1 grid/solar-irrigation village as a contrast. - Data collection: - a survey before planting (Nov) and at harvest (May), n~600 plots or farmers: tenure, planned vs actual area, irrigation arrangement and price, borrowing, household migration - interviews with pump owners and water sellers about how they set prices - focus groups with women in farming households on food and labour trade-offs - Administrative data: Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE) Boro area targets vs actuals, and BADC diesel/pump data if accessible.

Why it’s important. This is the channel most likely to turn into a 2027 food-price and forex problem. Almost no one is watching it yet (see §4c of the README).

Budget. ~$20–35k.
