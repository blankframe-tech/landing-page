**Written:** 1 October 2026. It updates [`impact_analysis.md`](impact_analysis.md), which was written on 21 Sep 2026.
**Convention:** Every factual claim gives its outlet, date and URL. **[est.]** marks this document's own calculation, and the method is shown next to it. "Not confirmed" means I searched in English and Bangla and found nothing reliable. It does not mean the thing did not happen.

## 0. The ten-day picture in five lines

1. **There was no second hike and no duty cut.** October pump prices are unchanged at diesel Tk 135, petrol Tk 160, octane Tk 165 and kerosene Tk 155. The government explicitly chose not to cut fuel taxes.
2. **The "October LC cliff" has been pushed back, not removed.** On 29 Sep the Finance Division gave BPC a **Tk 4,500 crore interest-free loan**, the first in 11 years. That covers roughly 7–8 weeks of the diesel loss run-rate **[est.]**.
3. **Pass-through is now visible and larger than the gazette.** The gazetted bus fare rose 17 paisa/km (+6.7% metro). Realised fares on many trips rose Tk 5–20 in Dhaka and Tk 50–100 on long-distance routes. Truck charters rose **~19–40% per trip**. Green chilli, eggplant, flour, eggs, milk and lentils all rose within a week.
4. **Furnace oil rose again (+Tk 11.92 to Tk 108.78/L from 1 Oct).** Qatar extended LNG force majeure to Asian buyers through November. The power cost base is climbing again going into winter.
5. **Officials now say no further hike is planned.** The State Minister said so on 23 Sep, and so did the BPC chairman on 29 Sep. Against that, the State Minister also said the full formula would put diesel at **~Tk 205**, which leaves a gap of about Tk 70/L. It does not narrow it.

### 1c. Duty/VAT cut: considered and declined
- The government declined a fuel tax cut. Fuel carries about **32% in cumulative duties and taxes**, levied on invoice value, so revenue rises automatically with world prices. NBR fuel-tax collections rose **~90% in the first two months of FY27** (from ~Tk 2,000 crore to ~Tk 3,800 crore). TBS ties the refusal to the pay-scale cost (Tk 1.05 lakh crore over 17 months). Zahid Hussain: "The government has shifted from formula-based policy to decision-based policy." TBS reports that at least 55 countries used direct fiscal measures. Source: TBS, 23 Sep 2026, https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/energy/govt-shies-away-fuel-tax-cut-amid-pay-hike-1551121
- Experts (M Shamsul Alam of CAB, Khondaker Golam Moazzem of CPD, Selim Raihan of SANEM) called for cutting fuel import taxes. The structure is **6% CD + 15% VAT + 2% AT + 2% AIT ≈ 25% on diesel imports**. Tk 10,555 crore was collected from diesel, crude and petrol imports in Jan–Aug 2026, of which diesel was Tk 8,042 crore. Source: The Daily Star, 22 Sep 2026, https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/experts-call-cutting-fuel-import-taxes-4279421
- An op-ed puts taxes at **~Tk 38 of the claimed Tk 52/L diesel "subsidy"** and proposes a fixed per-litre tax, citing Pakistan, Nepal and India, and Turkey's automatic stabiliser. Its Boro estimate is +3% cost per bigha, or ~7% in high-diesel areas, with no method given. Source: Daily Waadaa (op-ed), 24 Sep 2026, https://www.dailywaadaa.com/promissory-note/2026/09/24/a-regressive-inflationary-tax-masked-as-a-subsidy
- *Note:* the tax burden estimates now differ by source: Tk 32.44/L in the original analysis, ~25% (Daily Star), ~32% (TBS) and ~Tk 38/L (Daily Waadaa). They are not measured on the same basis. P5 should use NBR's own per-litre figure.

### 1d. BPC financing and LC status
- **The Finance Division approved a Tk 4,500 crore interest-free loan to BPC**, notified by letter on 29 Sep. Terms: repayment in half-yearly instalments over 5 years, including a 6-month grace period. It comes from the FY2025-26 operational-loan budget. It is the first such loan in 11 years. BPC had warned it could run out of LC funds in October. **State Minister Anindya Islam Amit: diesel "would have cost about Tk 205 a litre if the automatic pricing mechanism had been fully applied."** BPC also needs Tk 690 crore by December for the SPM loan, plus ITFC payments. Source: The Daily Star, 30 Sep 2026, https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/after-11yrs-bpc-gets-tk-4500cr-loan-buy-fuel-4286591
- Background: between 11 Mar and 8 Sep BPC sent 10 letters to the ministry; the last one asked for PMO attention. BPC Chairman Md Rafiqul Islam said opening LCs for October would be "difficult" without intervention. FY2025-26 fuel imports were a record **$10.63 bn (+107%)**. Source: Ajker Patrika (Bangla), 21 Sep 2026, https://www.ajkerpatrika.com/business/ajpf8q18umzou
- BPC chairman on 29 Sep: "we will try with our capacity so that prices never have to rise again." He said finances had been discussed with the Finance Ministry and were at a "satisfactory position", stocks were **~45 days**, and **procurement was complete through December**. Source: Bangla TV (minor outlet, not corroborated by a major outlet in my searches), 29 Sep 2026, https://banglatv.tv/news/2026/09/110073/
- **Coverage [est.]:** Tk 4,500 crore ÷ ~Tk 85 crore/day post-hike diesel loss (the impact_analysis estimate) ≈ **53 days**. As a share of the Tk 15,000–20,000 crore extra working capital BPC says it needs: **22.5–30%**. So the next BPC cash crunch plausibly falls in **late November–December**, the same window as Boro irrigation demand. This is an inference from the loan size and the loss run-rate, not a reported date.
- **Warning about a false lead:** search engines return a widely syndicated story that BPC had "$670 m overdue as of 30 September" ($260 m ITFC, $410 m others). **That story is from 3 Oct 2023** (TBS, https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/energy/urgent-dollar-support-sought-fuel-import-711030). It is not 2026 data, so do not use it. I could not confirm any 2026 figure for overdue BPC dollar payments.

## 5. Implications for each study

### P1 (fuel-shock panel): W0 timing
- **No new shock landed in the window, and the next scheduled decision point is ~30–31 Oct**, when the price for November is announced. The BPC loan makes an October emergency hike less likely. Officials publicly rule out further hikes, but the formula gap has *widened* (Tk 205 vs Tk 135) and Brent rose in September. **Recommendation: keep the 20 Oct W0 completion target. Treat 30 Oct as a hard stop**, because the November price notice comes out that day or the next. Record each interview's date so that any respondent after an announcement can be flagged.
- W0 is now **explicitly a post-September-hike, pre-next-shock baseline, not a pre-shock baseline.** The fare notification (22 Sep), freight jumps and food price rises have already happened.
