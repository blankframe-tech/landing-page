ThrottleIQ

Machine memory for motorcycles. An offline-first ride tracker, garage and rider community, in English and Bangla. It records every ride, remembers what each bike needs next, and connects riders.

Pre-launch betaFlutter + Firebase

Product pageBlankFrame brand
Crash detection and emergency alerting are built but not live. The brand page describes the design only, not safety features.

The look

An instrument panel. The dark theme is near-black with a lime primary and a magenta secondary, and everything numeric is set in a monospace face, the way a dashboard sets speed and lean angle. The light theme is the opposite mood: warm cream, a sage primary and a tan secondary, for use in daylight.

Shape and behaviour

Gauges and charts do the talking: a dual lean-angle arc, a G-force friction circle, per-ride and lifetime charts. The bike-colour picker offers twelve named paint colours (racing red, ocean teal, gunmetal grey) so each bike looks like its owner’s.

Shared and its own

Shared with BlankFrame

  • Same philosophy: the product carries context so the rider doesn’t repeat it
  • Two themes that follow the system setting, like the BlankFrame tokens
  • Bilingual from the start, English and Bangla

Its own

  • Lime and magenta replace BlankFrame blue as the accent
  • Monospace IBM Plex instead of Inter Tight and Instrument Serif
  • Palette lives in Dart (AppColorPalette), not in the shared token file

How it fits the philosophy

The best infrastructure is invisible. We build it, it disappears, and what’s left is your time.

BlankFrame builds “infrastructure that carries context, so you don’t have to repeat yourself.” ThrottleIQ is that sentence applied to a motorcycle: the app remembers every ride and what the bike needs next, so the rider doesn’t keep a notebook. The design follows the same rule. The interface is a quiet instrument panel that shows numbers while you ride and stays out of the way, and the work happens in the background with GPS and sensors, offline.
