Caffeinate

Keep your Mac awake for exactly as long as you need. A native macOS menu bar app: set a timer or tell it to wait for your AI agents, and your Mac stays awake, lid closed if you need it, then goes back to sleep.

BlankFrame brand

The look

It has almost no look of its own. It is a coffee cup in the menu bar with a live countdown next to it, no Dock icon, and system controls inside the popover. Colour, type and dark mode come from macOS.

Type

Everything
San Francisco (system)

Native SwiftUI, nothing custom

Shape and behaviour

Presets and one-click actions (30 minutes, an hour, a 45-minute commute with the lid closed), and safety stops that end a session if the Mac gets hot or the battery drops to 15%. The mark is the SF Symbol cup.and.saucer.

Shared and its own

Shared with BlankFrame

  • Does one job and gets out of the way
  • Defaults over options
  • Honest limits written into the app and the README

Its own

  • It follows Apple’s interface, not the BlankFrame tokens, on purpose: a menu bar app that looked different would feel wrong
  • Open source, MIT licence
