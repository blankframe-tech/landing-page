Is this taka note real?

Pick your note, then go through the checks: feel it, tilt it, hold it to the light. A forger can copy one or two features. A real note has all of them.

New series · ৳1000, ৳100, ৳50, ৳20

1. Feel it

Run your thumb over the front of the note. No tools needed.

2. Tilt it

Move the note back and forth under a light and watch the colours change.

3. Hold it up to the light

Look through the note at a window or a bulb.

4. With a magnifier or UV light

A magnifying glass, a phone camera zoomed in, or the UV detector at a shop or bank.

If a check fails

  • Compare it side by side with a note you got from a bank or ATM. Most fakes look wrong next to a real one.
  • Don’t pass it on. Take it to a bank branch and ask the teller to check it.
  • This list covers the new series only (governor Ahsan H. Mansur’s signature, theme “Historical and Archaeological Architecture of Bangladesh”). Older notes have different features: see Bangladesh Bank’s note guide.
